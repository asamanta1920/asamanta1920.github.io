What I Do

Research

I work on computational biology and distributed systems across multiple labs. At MIT CSAIL & the Broad Institute, I study cell-cell communication in Alzheimer's disease using snRNA-seq data, apply spatial transcriptomics with 10X Visium, and train protein language models. Through MIT PRIMES, I optimize microservice performance using reinforcement learning. I also study coral genomics at Tufts and learning patterns in biological systems in Prof. Levin's lab.

Education

Senior at Greater Commonwealth Virtual School (GPA: 4.76). Dual enrollment at Greenfield Community College (4.0 GPA) and Tufts University Precollege Program (4.0 GPA), taking courses in Real Analysis, Abstract Algebra, Algorithms, Network Science, Computation Theory, and more.

Teaching & Outreach

Previously developed the RACECAR-Zero platform, a $125 alternative to MIT's $1000 RACECAR autonomous vehicle, and taught it across multiple Boston-area schools and at Tufts University.
